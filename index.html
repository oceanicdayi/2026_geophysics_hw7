aCalculate the:

  1. theoretical gravity;
  2. free air correction;
  3. Bouguer correction;
  4. free air gravity anomaly; and
  5. Bouguer gravity anomaly.

bSuppose that the gravity station in (a) had exactly the same latitude, longitude and observed gravity, but it was located on the surface of the ocean, over a water depth of 487.9 m. Recompute the free-air and Bouguer gravity anomalies.
