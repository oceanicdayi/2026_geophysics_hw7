## Problem 8-2

Given the following data for a gravity station:

- Latitude: 48.1195° N
- Longitude: 12.1878° E
- Elevation: 487.9 m
- Observed gravity: 980,717.39 mGal

a) Calculate the: i) theoretical gravity; ii) free air correction; iii) Bouguer correction;
iv) free air gravity anomaly; and v) Bouguer gravity anomaly.

b) Suppose that the gravity station in (a) had exactly the same latitude, longitude and
observed gravity, but it was located on the surface of the ocean, over a water depth of
487.9 m. Recompute the free-air and Bouguer gravity anomalies.
